Return policy: the chatbot invented a 30-day policy that does not exist

Customer asked

“What is your return policy?”

Chatbot answered — wrong

The true policy — verified

Our return policy allows returns within 14 days of purchase.

Remove the claim. Correct fact: returns within 14 days of purchase.
